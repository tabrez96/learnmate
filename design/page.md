# Learnmate

## The problem

Your child's school material is everywhere: notebooks, handouts, PDFs and Google Classroom. There's no daily plan for revising it, no way to know what actually stuck, and when you hand over the tablet you can't tell whether studying really happened.

## How it helps

- A friendly mentor teaches today's topic step by step, using only your child's own notes and school documents unless they choose to check outside them.
- Every study session ends with a short test, and the grade is sent to you on Telegram.
- You start the session and only you can end it, with a PIN. If your child leaves the app or stops working, you hear about it.
- Points, streaks and small challenges make coming back every day feel good.

## Who it's for

- Parents of children in grades 5 to 10 who want daily revision to happen, and proof that it did.
- Students who learn better with a patient mentor and a quick test than with a pile of notes.
