# Learnmate: guide

## Getting started

1. Get Learnmate on your child's Android tablet. It isn't in the app stores yet: whoever sets up Learnmate for your family installs it for you, and it is ready to open.
2. Open Learnmate and type your child's first name and grade. (S01)
3. Connect Telegram, so grades, alerts and session PINs reach your phone. (S01)
4. Choose a fallback password of at least 8 characters and type it twice. It is your last resort if a session PIN ever stops working, and it is never sent to Telegram, so keep it somewhere safe. Then tap "Finish setup". (S01)
5. Optional: open Settings, add your child's subjects, like Science or Maths, and add their notes ahead of time with "+ Add material". You can also do this on the day. (S09)

## How to use it

**Start today's study session**

1. On the home screen, tap "Start today's session". (S01)
2. Pick the subject, then tick the material to learn from. To add something new, upload a photo, PDF or Word file, or paste a Google Classroom link. You can name the chapters to focus on. Tap "Continue". (S02)
3. Check the summary and note the session PIN. It is new for every session and is also sent to your Telegram. Tap "Start session and hand over" and give the tablet to your child. (S03)
4. Your child follows today's plan: a warm-up, a lesson with the mentor, then the test. (S04, S05, S06)
5. After the test your child sees the score and what to review, and the grade is sent to your Telegram. (S07)
6. When your child is done, enter the session PIN to end the session and see the summary: time studied, grade, points, and any times they left Learnmate. (S08)

While the session runs, you get a Telegram message if your child leaves Learnmate. After 5 minutes with no activity the session locks, and you unlock it with the same PIN. (S11)

**Recap an earlier chapter, or give a test only**

1. Tap "Start today's session". (S01)
2. For a recap, tap "Nothing new today: recap an earlier chapter", pick the subject and one of its chapters. For a test without a lesson, choose "Test only" at the top, pick the subject, tick one or more chapters and choose 5, 10 or 15 questions. Tap "Continue". (S02)
3. Note the session PIN and hand over the tablet as usual. (S03)

**If you forget the PIN**

1. On the end session screen, tap "Forgot the PIN? Send it to Telegram". The same PIN arrives on your phone. (S08)
2. If the PIN still doesn't work after 3 tries, tap "PIN not working? Use the fallback password" and enter your fallback password to end the session. (S08)
